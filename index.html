Canadian firsts

Answers with a story behind them

Many Canuckle answers point to people, places and ideas that started in Canada. A few examples you might meet on the board:

HOOPS

Basketball was invented in 1891 by James Naismith, born in Almonte, Ontario.

PHONE

Alexander Graham Bell worked on the telephone at his family home in Brantford, Ontario.

POPPY

Canadian doctor John McCrae wrote “In Flanders Fields”, which made the poppy a symbol of remembrance.

APPLE

The McIntosh apple was discovered on John McIntosh’s farm in Ontario in 1811.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What time does the daily Canuckle change?

On this page the daily word changes at midnight in your own time zone, and a countdown in the results window shows how long is left.

What is a good Canuckle score?

Solving in four guesses is a solid result, three is excellent and two usually involves some luck. Any win within six guesses keeps your streak alive.

Are French words used in Canuckle?

Answers are English or widely used Canadian words. Where a word comes from French or an Indigenous language, it is written without accents.

Why did my streak reset?

A streak counts days in a row on which you solved the daily word. Missing a day or failing a puzzle starts the count again. Practice rounds never affect it.

Is Canuckle good for classrooms?

Yes. The Canadian theme and the fun fact after each round make it a quick warm-up for spelling, vocabulary and Canadian studies.

Do I need to sign up?

No. The game runs in your browser and your statistics are saved on your own device.
